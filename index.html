Food safety, taken seriously

AI in the kitchen needs guardrails. Ours are structural.

Most AI food tools generate text and hope. FoodML treats allergen policy as a hard constraint and treats AI writes as proposals — because a menu system that can quietly serve the wrong dish is worse than no system at all.

🚫 Hard allergen constraints

Banned allergens are enforced at four gates — readiness checks, recipe search, and both AI write paths — so the agent never even sees a non-compliant dish. One policy, read by frontend and backend alike, so the gates can’t drift apart.

policy: fail-closed · gates: 4 · drift: impossible

🧪 Second-line screening

Declared allergens aren’t trusted blindly. An independent ingredient-text screener — backed by a 48-test guard suite — re-checks everything AI-generated, catching under-declared dishes before they reach a menu.

screen_allergens · selftest: 48 guards

✋ Propose, then apply

The AutoMenu agent reads live data freely but stages every write as a proposed action. A chef reviews and applies — and applied actions go through the same capability-gated paths as a human user. No silent AI writes, ever.

reads: live · writes: staged → chef-approved
